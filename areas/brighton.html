Brighton, Marino and Hallett Cove offer family-friendly southern beaches with less tourist density than Glenelg or West Beach — a tradeoff that suits a specific owner profile well. Summer demand is genuine, event peaks around LIV Golf and Adelaide Fringe push rates up meaningfully, and the December-to-February corridor fills reliably. But the corridor's real advantage is stability: lower volatility than the busier beachside zones means occupancy is more predictable outside peak windows, and owners who want steadier, less volatile returns rather than chasing maximum yield at the height of summer tend to find this stretch performs more consistently to expectation.

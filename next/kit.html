Your home

Can I use my own home?

Yes. Your dates are blocked so guests cannot book them. At the coast and in the Hills the summer block is kept to one or two weeks, because summer carries the year in those places.

Do you take apartments and townhouses?

Yes, where the body corporate allows short stays. That is the first thing to check, because a by-law prohibiting short-term letting rules the home out whatever it is like. Sleeping capacity also needs to match the bedroom count. A three bedroom apartment or townhouse should sleep six.

One turnover, hour by hour

One guest leaves at 10 and the next arrives at 3. These are the five hours in between.

  1. 10.00 am

    Guest out, cleaners in

    Check-out is 10 am and the clean starts then, with fresh hired linen on every bed.

  2. 12.30 pm

    The photos arrive

    The cleaners send about 35 photos of the finished home, with a short report on beds, linen, amenities and damage.

  3. 1.00 pm

    The photos are checked

    Every clean is checked the same day against the cleaner's photos.

  4. Regularly

    A walkthrough, in person

    We walk through in person regularly, and always when something looks off. The same 11 checks, in the same order, every time.

    See the 11 checks
    1. Corners, skirting, under beds and tables
    2. Walls and doors, for scuffs and chips
    3. Drains in the shower, basin and floor
    4. Grout and silicone, for mould starting
    5. Amenities, counted
    6. Inside the cupboards guests use
    7. Remotes, lamps, batteries and bulbs
    8. Windows, tracks and flyscreens
    9. Outdoor furniture, ash and bins
    10. The smell at the front door
    11. Anything moved or missing against the listing photos

  5. 2.00 pm

    Faults are written up

    Anything wrong is photographed and a trade is booked. If it is yours to know, you get a note.

  6. 3.00 pm

    Guest checks in

    Check-in opens at 3 pm. A clean is never rushed to meet it. If one runs over, the guest waits.

Times are typical for a same-day changeover. The order does not change.

Cost

How much does Airbnb management cost in Adelaide?

Adelaide managers typically quote between 15% and 25% of gross booking revenue, and the base they use varies. Aurea charges on net accommodation revenue, which is the booking total after the platform's commission. Tier 1 is 24% of net plus a one-off $2,200 onboarding fee. Tier 2 is 26% of net with no onboarding fee. On a gross basis that is roughly 20% and 22%. Both include GST. Guests pay the cleaning fee on each stay, and professional photography and the initial setup clean are the only other costs, invoiced at cost. Full detail is on our fees page.

What is net accommodation revenue?

Net accommodation revenue is the gross booking revenue for a stay, less the booking platform's commission on that revenue. The management fee is charged on that figure, and on nothing else. If a booking takes $1,000 in accommodation revenue and the platform commission is $150, net accommodation revenue is $850, so the Tier 1 management fee is 24% of $850, which is $204. Cleaning fees sit outside the calculation.

Who pays the cleaning fee?

The guest pays the cleaning fee, on every stay. It is charged as a separate line item on the booking, collected by Aurea, and paid to the cleaning provider that turns the property over. It does not form part of net accommodation revenue and no management fee is charged on it, so it does not come out of the owner's share.

What does the owner still pay?

The running costs of owning the property stay with the owner, as they would with any property. These are utilities, insurance, council rates, and maintenance. Aurea coordinates maintenance and engages the tradespeople, and those costs are invoiced to the owner at cost. Two setup items are also an owner cost on both tiers: professional photography and the initial setup clean, both invoiced at cost with no margin, markup or coordination charge added. The detail is set out on the fees page.

Your home

Which parts of Adelaide do you manage?

Aurea manages homes across metropolitan Adelaide. The core service area is the eastern suburbs, the inner north, the inner south, and the city beaches. Adelaide Hills and Fleurieu homes run as short stays from October to February and take mid-term placements from March to September, which matches the seasonal demand pattern in those areas rather than running them as year-round short-stay listings. Aurea does not manage properties interstate. There is more on where we work.

Can I use my own home?

Yes. Your dates are blocked so guests cannot book them. At the coast and in the Hills the summer block is kept to one or two weeks, because summer carries the year in those places.

Do you take homes for a few months only?

No. A three or four month stint finishes before the listing has built the review history that a nightly rate is set on, so the home is handed back at the point it would start performing. A furnished mid-term let arranged through a rental agent suits that timeframe better.

Do you take apartments and townhouses?

Yes, where the body corporate allows short stays. That is the first thing to check, because a by-law prohibiting short-term letting rules the home out whatever it is like. Sleeping capacity also needs to match the bedroom count. A three bedroom apartment or townhouse should sleep six.

Guests and care

How are guests screened?

Every guest has their identity verified before a door code is issued. Bookings that do not fit the home are declined, including parties and groups the home is not set up to hold.

How fast do guests get an answer?

Guests get a reply within minutes, 7 days a week.

Who checks the clean?

Every clean is checked the same day against the cleaner's photos. We walk through in person regularly, and always when something looks off.

Why do you keep the portfolio small?

Because the work is in the detail. Each home is priced by hand and every clean is checked, and that is only possible with a small portfolio and a dedicated team. Staying small is the plan, not a stage of growth.

Getting started

What is the first step?

The first step is a call. Send your details and the address through the contact page and we will call you back, usually the same business day. On the call we talk through the home and the nightly rates similar homes nearby are achieving.

Will you send an earnings estimate?

No. Every home is different, and a figure given before we know yours would be a guess. We talk nightly rates on the phone once we know the home. We do not forecast annual income.

Money and statements

What happens over Christmas and events?

Rates for Christmas, the Adelaide Fringe, WOMADelaide and Gather Round are set ahead of the season and reviewed through it as bookings come in, rather than left on a default calendar.

How do I receive my earnings?

Owners receive a statement every month within ten business days of month end. It shows bookings, platform commission, the management fee and the owner share, and the owner share is paid with it.

Can I see my bookings?

Yes. Every owner has a real-time dashboard that shows bookings as they land.

For owners

What you receive

Three documents and one dashboard, set out the way an owner gets them. Names and amounts are left blank.

A note after a stay

Sent after a stay, and only when there is something to tell you. It says what was found, shows the photo and says whether you need to do anything.

No note means nothing needed your attention.

A fault note

When something breaks you get one email. What it is, what happens next and whether you need to act.

The trail at the bottom shows where it is up to. Found, booked, told, fixed.

A statement every month

It arrives within ten business days of month end and your share is paid with it.

  • Accommodation fare

    What guests paid for the nights.

  • Less platform commission

    What the booking site keeps.

  • Net accommodation revenue

    The figure our fee is charged on.

  • Less management fee

    Our fee.

  • Less expenses paid for you

    Trades and items we paid for on your behalf, at cost.

  • Paid to you

    The balance, paid with the statement.

How each one is checked

Your bookings, as they land

Every owner has a real-time dashboard. Open it at any time and see your bookings as they land. You do not have to ask us or wait for the statement.

What we do not send

An earnings estimate before we have spoken. Every home is different, and a figure given before we know yours would be a guess. We talk nightly rates on the phone once we know the home.

Tell us about your home

We will call you back, usually the same business day.

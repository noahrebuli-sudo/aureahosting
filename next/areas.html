Areas

Where we work

Adelaide, the coast, the Hills and the Fleurieu. Who books in each and how the year runs there.

  • Eastern suburbs Norwood, Kensington, Beaumont, Glenside, Felixstow, Payneham and Magill. Family houses and townhouses, booked through the year.
  • Inner north Prospect, Nailsworth, Walkerville, Medindie, Fitzroy, Bowden and Brompton. Townhouses and renovated homes close to the city.
  • City Adelaide city and North Adelaide. Apartments and townhouses, where the body corporate allows short stays.
  • City beaches Glenelg, Henley Beach, Grange, Semaphore and Largs Bay. A summer market.
  • Southern beaches Brighton, Marino and Hallett Cove. A quieter stretch of the same coast.
  • Hills and Fleurieu Hahndorf, Stirling, Port Noarlunga, Aldinga and Normanville. Short stay in summer, mid-term placements through winter.

The inner south

Unley, Hyde Park, Malvern, Parkside, Mitcham and Westbourne Park run on the same year-round shape as the east. Character homes and townhouses, booked by families, corporate travellers and event visitors.

Seasonal homes run on two settings

From October to February a Hills or Fleurieu home is let as a short stay at holiday rates. From March to September it takes mid-term placements of two weeks to three months.

Owner summer use is kept to one or two weeks, because summer carries the year in these places.

Outside the area

We do not manage homes interstate. In South Australia we stop at the Adelaide Hills and the Fleurieu.

Keeping the map tight is what lets us walk through each home regularly. If your home sits outside these suburbs, say so on the first call.

The standard

How we run a home

Five rules, written down, and how each one is checked. Read them before you call and hold us to them after.

GuestsCleansMaintenancePricingWhat you hear from usGetting started

01

Guests

Guests get a reply within minutes, 7 days a week.

Messages from Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo and Stayz arrive in one inbox, so nothing sits unread on a site nobody is watching.

Every guest has their identity verified before a door code is issued. Bookings that do not fit the home are declined, including parties and groups the home is not set up to hold.

How it is checked One inbox for all four booking sites, every message timed.

02

Cleans

Checked after every clean. Walked through regularly.

The cleaners send about 35 photos of the finished home, with a short report on beds, linen, amenities and damage.

Every clean is checked the same day against the cleaner's photos. We walk through in person regularly, and always when something looks off.

The walk follows the same 11 checks, in the same order, every time.

See the 11 checks
  1. Corners, skirting, under beds and tables
  2. Walls and doors, for scuffs and chips
  3. Drains in the shower, basin and floor
  4. Grout and silicone, for mould starting
  5. Amenities, counted
  6. Inside the cupboards guests use
  7. Remotes, lamps, batteries and bulbs
  8. Windows, tracks and flyscreens
  9. Outdoor furniture, ash and bins
  10. The smell at the front door
  11. Anything moved or missing against the listing photos

How it is checked The cleaners' photos, the same day. A fixed list of 11 checks on the walk.

03

Maintenance

A fault is written up with a photo, and a trade is booked.

Small faults are found at the changeover, when they are still small.

You get a note that says what it is, what happens next and whether you need to act. We engage the trade and the cost is passed on to you at cost.

How it is checked A note to you with what it is, what happens next and whether you need to act.

04

Pricing

Rates are set by hand and reviewed every week.

Rates for Christmas, the Adelaide Fringe, WOMADelaide and Gather Round are set ahead of the season and reviewed as bookings come in.

One calendar feeds all four booking sites, so a booked night closes everywhere. We have had zero double bookings.

We do not forecast annual income. We price each night.

How it is checked One calendar for all four booking sites. Your bookings show on a real-time dashboard.

05

What you hear from us

A statement every month, and a note when something is yours to know.

The statement arrives within ten business days of month end and your share is paid with it.

Between statements you hear from us when something needs your eyes, and not otherwise.

How it is checked Three documents and a dashboard. See what you receive

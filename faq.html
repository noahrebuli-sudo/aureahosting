Common Questions

The questions owners actually ask.

Straight answers on Airbnb and short-term rental management in Adelaide — pricing, fees, timelines, and how Aurea actually works.

We charge a management fee based on your property's revenue. Our fee structure is transparent, with no hidden charges — you'll see every booking, every cost, and every dollar earned in your monthly statement. Contact us for a specific quote based on your property.

Most properties go live within 7 days of signing. Day 1 is assessment, Days 2–5 cover photography and listing creation, and Day 7 is live across all 50+ platforms.

Your property will be listed on 50+ channels including Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, Expedia, Google Travel, Tripadvisor, Stayz, Domain, and more — all managed through Guesty's centralised platform.

Yes. You have full control over owner-use dates through the owner portal. Simply block any dates you want to use the property yourself and they'll be unavailable for guest bookings.

We use a combination of market data, Adelaide's event calendar, competitor rates, and demand signals to set optimal nightly rates. During high-demand periods like WomAdelaide, Adelaide Fringe, or Gather Round, rates are adjusted to capture maximum revenue.

Our team handles all guest issues 24/7. For damage, we manage the full claims process on your behalf — documenting, reporting, and following up with the relevant platform and insurance provider.

Earnings are transferred monthly after deduction of our management fee and any approved property costs. You'll receive a full statement detailing every booking, rate, and deduction.

We look for quality properties in Adelaide's key short-term rental corridors — beachside, city fringe, and Hills regions. Properties should be well-presented and ideally achieve a nightly rate above the suburb median. The best way to find out is to apply — we'll give you an honest assessment.

Most properties are live within 7 days of signing, though this depends on the home being ready for photography. Here's how it typically works: Day 1 is onboarding. We collect all property information, arrange an initial inspection if we haven't seen the home yet, and book the first clean, linen hire, and photography. Day 2 the cleaners attend. Photography follows with around a 3 to 4 day lead time, and once photos are returned (usually within 48 hours) we build the listing and go live the same day.

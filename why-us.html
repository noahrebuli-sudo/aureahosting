Yield Impact

What this means
for your property

50+ channels, live-synced

Your property reaches guests who never open Airbnb: corporate bookers on Booking.com, international travellers on Expedia, and domestic guests on Domain and Stayz. Each additional channel is occupancy you wouldn't otherwise capture. Most self-managed listings run on 1–2 platforms and leave the rest of the market untouched.

Event-aware pricing engine

During Adelaide Fringe, Gather Round, or LIV Golf, rates don't adjust when demand peaks. They adjust 3–5 days before. Guests who book early pay more. Late bookers pay more still. A fixed-rate listing misses both ends. The revenue gap is most visible in the two weeks surrounding each major Adelaide event.

Unified calendar, real-time sync

A booking on Booking.com closes your Airbnb availability within seconds. Managing even three platforms manually creates double-booking risk. Across 50+ channels, that risk is not recoverable without automation. Double-booking incidents across our current portfolio: zero.

Owner dashboard, live data

Revenue, occupancy, and booking pace are visible in real-time, not buried in a monthly PDF sent three weeks after the fact. If a week is underperforming against forecast, you see it before the month closes. So do we. Variance gets addressed, not reported retroactively.

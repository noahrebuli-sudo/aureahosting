About Aurea

The people behind your property

Aurea is founder-led and deliberately small. Here's who you're dealing with.

Noah Rebuli, Founder

Aurea was founded on five years of managing short-stay properties across Adelaide: more than twenty homes, thousands of guest stays, every kind of property and every kind of problem. What that experience taught us is simple: the difference between an average return and a great one is care, taken daily, and care doesn't scale to hundreds of doors.

So Aurea stays deliberately small. We cap the portfolio so every home gets the attention it deserves, and every owner deals directly with Noah, the founder, not a rotating roster of account managers. Your calls go to the person who priced your calendar this morning and walked your hallway after the last clean.

Behind that sits a hand-picked operation: cleaning crews held to a written standard, professional photography, quality linen, and serious technology working around the clock on pricing, guest communication and records. Small where it matters, sophisticated where it counts.

The Standard

How we run things

Worked daily

Pricing moves every day with Adelaide's events, seasons and demand. A rate set in January and forgotten is money left on the table, so nothing at Aurea runs on autopilot.

Checked in person

Every clean is followed by a walkthrough before the next guest arrives. Photos, presentation, the small things guests notice, verified in person, never assumed.

Built the modern way

Aurea runs on systems most managers twice our size don't have, doing the heavy lifting on pricing, records and guest communication, so the founder's time goes where it matters: your home and your guests.

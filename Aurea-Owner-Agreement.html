Owner Management Agreement

Owner Management Agreement

OMA-v1.2

6IX SERVICES PTY LTD (ABN 15 669 346 249) trading as Aurea Hosting

This Agreement is to be read in conjunction with the Owner Appointment Form signed by the Owner.

1. Parties

This Agreement is made between:

(a) 6IX SERVICES PTY LTD (ABN 15 669 346 249) trading as Aurea Hosting, of Adelaide, South Australia (the "Manager"); and

(b) The property owner identified in the signed Owner Appointment Form (the "Owner"), together referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party".

The property managed under this Agreement is the short-term rental property described in the Owner Appointment Form (the "Property").

2. Definitions

In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

  • "Net Accommodation Revenue" means the gross accommodation revenue for a booking, less (a) the booking platform fees attributable to that accommodation revenue (including but not limited to Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, and Stayz service fees); and less (b) any Excess Cleaning Costs for that booking under clause 10.4. Cleaning Fees do not form part of gross accommodation revenue. They are not added to it and are not deducted from it. For the avoidance of doubt, Net Accommodation Revenue does not include any linen charges or other pass-through expenses.
  • "Management Fee" means the percentage fee payable to the Manager, calculated on Net Accommodation Revenue, as specified in clause 8 of this Agreement. All Management Fee rates stated in this Agreement are inclusive of GST.
  • "Onboarding Fee" means the one-off fee of $2,200 (incl. GST) payable by the Owner under Tier 1 upon commencement of this Agreement, covering listing creation and optimisation, pricing configuration, and preparation of guest materials, as specified in clause 8.3. The Onboarding Fee does not include professional photography or the Initial Setup Costs, which are an Owner cost invoiced separately at cost under clause 8.4. The Onboarding Fee is inclusive of GST and is non-refundable.
  • "Initial Setup Costs" means the cost of professional photography of the Property and the initial setup clean or deep clean of the Property prior to the listing going live. Initial Setup Costs are an Owner cost, are coordinated by the Manager, and are invoiced to the Owner separately at cost. They are not covered by the Onboarding Fee or the Management Fee, and are payable regardless of the fee tier selected.
  • "Guest Consumables" means the consumable guest supplies described in clause 9, which are supplied and paid for by the Manager at no cost to the Owner.
  • "Cleaning Fee" means the fee charged to guests as a separate line item on each booking to cover professional cleaning and turnover of the Property between stays. The Cleaning Fee is collected by the Manager and applied to the cost of cleaning the Property; it does not form part of Net Accommodation Revenue and is not commissionable. For clarity, the Cleaning Fee is distinct from the Initial Setup Costs.
  • "Excess Cleaning Costs" means the amount by which the actual cost of cleaning a turnover exceeds the Cleaning Fee collected for that booking, as described in clause 10.4.
  • "Initial Term" means the period of 12 months commencing on the date the Owner Appointment Form is signed by the Owner.
  • "Renewal Term" means each subsequent period of 12 months following the expiry of the Initial Term or any preceding Renewal Term, as provided for in clause 4.
  • "Booking Platform" means any online marketplace or channel through which the Property is listed for short-term rental, including but not limited to Airbnb, Booking.com, Vrbo, and Stayz.
  • "GST" has the meaning given in the A New Tax System (Goods and Services Tax) Act 1999 (Cth). All fees, rates, and dollar amounts stated in this Agreement are inclusive of GST unless expressly stated otherwise.

3. Appointment

3.1 The Owner appoints the Manager as its exclusive agent and manager for the purpose of marketing, listing, and managing the short-term rental of the Property on Booking Platforms for guest accommodation.

3.2 The Manager accepts the appointment and agrees to perform the services set out in this Agreement with reasonable skill, care, and diligence.

3.3 During the term of this Agreement, the Owner must not appoint any other person or entity to provide short-term rental management services for the Property, nor list or manage the Property on any Booking Platform independently, without the prior written consent of the Manager.

4. Term & Renewal

4.1 This Agreement commences on the date the Owner Appointment Form is signed by the Owner and continues for the Initial Term of 12 months.

4.2 At the expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement will automatically renew for successive Renewal Terms of 12 months each, unless terminated in accordance with clause 14.

4.3 For the avoidance of doubt, this Agreement does not convert to a month-to-month arrangement after the Initial Term. Each Renewal Term is a further fixed period of 12 months.

5. Manager's Responsibilities

The Manager agrees to provide the following services in connection with the short-term rental management of the Property:

  • Listing creation & optimisation: Create, publish, and maintain optimised listings across relevant Booking Platforms, including compelling descriptions, accurate amenity details, and strategic positioning.
  • Dynamic pricing management: Set and adjust nightly rates using data-driven pricing tools, market analysis, and seasonal demand patterns to maximise occupancy and revenue.
  • Guest communication: Handle all guest enquiries, booking requests, pre-arrival information, in-stay support, and post-stay follow-up. The Manager will respond to guest communications promptly, and will treat matters affecting guest safety or security as urgent.
  • Booking management: Manage all reservations, calendar synchronisation across platforms, and guest check-in/check-out coordination.
  • Cleaning & linen turnover: Coordinate professional cleaning and linen services between every guest stay, ensuring the Property is cleaned to a professional standard.
  • Guest Consumables: Supply and bear the cost of all Guest Consumables in accordance with clause 9, and replenish them at each guest turnover.
  • Property inspections: Conduct a formal property inspection at least once every 3 months, with a written report provided to the Owner within 7 days of each inspection.
  • Monthly reporting: Provide the Owner with a monthly statement within 10 business days of month-end detailing booking activity, revenue, expenses, occupancy rates, and net disbursements, in accordance with clause 11.
  • Maintenance coordination: Coordinate repairs and maintenance for the Property, engaging qualified tradespeople as needed. The Manager may authorise emergency repairs up to $150 (incl. GST) without prior Owner approval. Repairs exceeding $150 require Owner approval before works commence, except where clause 6.6 applies.
  • Photography & setup coordination: Coordinate professional photography and the initial setup clean of the Property. These are Initial Setup Costs and are an Owner cost invoiced separately at cost under clause 8.4.

6. Owner's Responsibilities

The Owner agrees to:

6.1 Maintain the Property in a safe, clean, habitable, and guest-ready condition at all times, including all furnishings, appliances, fixtures, and fittings. For clarity, this obligation does not extend to Guest Consumables, which are supplied by the Manager under clause 9.

6.2 Supply and replace the durable items listed in clause 9.2 at the Owner's cost.

6.3 Maintain appropriate and current insurance coverage for the Property, including building insurance, contents insurance, public liability insurance, and short-term rental or landlord insurance. The Owner must provide evidence of current insurance to the Manager upon request.

6.4 Comply with all applicable laws, regulations, local council requirements, and strata or body corporate by-laws relating to the use of the Property for short-term rental accommodation.

6.5 Respond to maintenance requests and approval requests from the Manager within 48 hours. If the Owner fails to respond within 48 hours, the Manager may proceed with maintenance that is reasonably necessary to preserve guest safety or prevent further damage to the Property.

6.6 Urgent Repairs Affecting a Current or Imminent Booking

Where a fault or failure at the Property is reasonably likely to result in a guest refund, compensation payment, booking cancellation, or a materially adverse guest review, the following applies in place of the 48-hour period in clause 6.5:

  • (a) The Manager will notify the Owner of the fault and the estimated cost of repair, by phone and in writing, and will mark the request as urgent.
  • (b) If the Owner does not respond within 6 hours of that notification, the Manager may authorise the repair without further approval, up to a maximum of $500 (incl. GST) per incident.
  • (c) Repairs reasonably estimated to exceed $500 continue to require the Owner's approval, except where the repair is necessary to preserve guest safety or prevent further damage to the Property.
  • (d) Costs authorised under this clause are pass-through expenses payable by the Owner and will appear on the next monthly statement. They are not subject to the Management Fee.
  • (e) The Manager will provide the Owner with the invoice and a short written explanation of the fault and the action taken within 48 hours of the repair being authorised.

6.7 Provide the Manager with at least 14 days' written notice of any intended personal use of the Property. Personal use blocks will be entered into the booking calendar and may not displace confirmed guest bookings.

6.8 Not interfere with or contact guests directly regarding bookings managed under this Agreement, unless agreed in writing with the Manager.

7. Owner Availability

7.1 The Owner must make the Property available for short-stay bookings for a minimum of 70% of any calendar month during the term of this Agreement.

7.2 Personal use, owner blocks, or other unavailability must not exceed 30% of any calendar month, unless otherwise agreed in writing between the Parties having regard to the specific circumstances of the Property.

7.3 Owner blocks must be communicated to the Manager with at least 14 days' written notice and may not displace confirmed guest bookings already accepted by the Manager.

8. Fees & Payment

8.1 Management Fee

The Owner will pay the Manager a Management Fee calculated as a percentage of Net Accommodation Revenue for each booking, at the tier selected by the Owner in the Owner Appointment Form:

  • Tier 1: Management Fee of 24% of Net Accommodation Revenue, plus a one-off Onboarding Fee of $2,200.
  • Tier 2: Management Fee of 26% of Net Accommodation Revenue, with no Onboarding Fee payable.

All Management Fee rates and the Onboarding Fee are inclusive of GST. Neither tier includes professional photography or the Initial Setup Costs, which are an Owner cost under clause 8.4.

8.2 How the Management Fee is Calculated

The Management Fee is calculated on Net Accommodation Revenue, as defined in clause 2. For the Owner's convenience, that calculation is:

  • (a) all Booking Platform fees, commissions, and service charges; and
  • (b) any Excess Cleaning Costs for that booking under clause 10.4.

This summary is illustrative. Where it differs from the definition in clause 2, clause 2 prevails.

The Management Fee is NOT charged on Cleaning Fees, linen charges, or any other pass-through expenses. Guesty software fees and other back-end operational costs are absorbed by the Manager and are not charged separately to the Owner.

8.3 Onboarding Fee (Tier 1)

Where Tier 1 is selected, the Owner must pay the Onboarding Fee of $2,200 (incl. GST) upon execution of this Agreement. The Onboarding Fee covers listing creation and optimisation, pricing configuration, and preparation of guest welcome materials. It does not include professional photography or the Initial Setup Costs, which are governed by clause 8.4. The Onboarding Fee is:

  • (a) a one-off payment and is non-refundable under any circumstances, including termination;
  • (b) entirely separate from and in addition to any Early Termination Fee under clause 15; and
  • (c) payable regardless of the duration of this Agreement.

8.4 Photography & Initial Setup Costs (Owner Cost)

Professional photography and the initial setup clean or deep clean of the Property are an Owner cost. They are not covered by the Onboarding Fee and are not covered by the Management Fee. This applies to Tier 1 and Tier 2 equally.

8.4.1 The Manager will coordinate professional photography and the initial setup clean of the Property. The Manager engages the photographer and cleaning provider on the Owner's behalf.

8.4.2 These Initial Setup Costs are invoiced to the Owner separately, at cost. The Manager applies no margin, markup, commission, or coordination charge to them.

8.4.3 The Manager will provide the Owner with a written estimate of the Initial Setup Costs before engaging any provider.

8.4.4 Initial Setup Costs are payable by the Owner within 14 days of invoice. The Manager is not obliged to publish the listing until the photography and initial setup clean have been completed.

8.4.5 For the avoidance of doubt, the initial setup clean is distinct from the ongoing per-booking Cleaning Fee described in clause 10.

8.5 Payment Timing

Owner disbursements will be made monthly, typically within 10 business days of month-end, accompanied by the monthly statement described in clause 11. The Manager may deduct the Management Fee and any other amounts owing under this Agreement from booking revenue before disbursement to the Owner.

9. Guest Consumables

9.1 The Manager will supply and bear the cost of all Guest Consumables for the duration of this Agreement, at no cost to the Owner. Guest Consumables include coffee pods, tea, sugar, toiletries (soap, shampoo, conditioner, body wash), toilet paper, paper towel, bin liners, dishwashing liquid, dishwasher tablets, and laundry detergent.

9.2 The Owner is responsible for the supply and replacement of durable items, including mattresses, pillows, mattress protectors, pillow protectors, linen (where not covered by the rented linen component of the guest Cleaning Fee), crockery, cutlery, cookware, appliances, and furnishings.

9.3 Guest Consumables remain the property of the Manager and are supplied for guest use only.

9.4 The cost of Guest Consumables is borne by the Manager and is not a pass-through expense. It is not deducted from Net Accommodation Revenue, is not charged to the Owner, and does not appear as an expense on the monthly statement issued under clause 11.

9.5 The Manager will replenish Guest Consumables at each guest turnover so that the Property is presented to each incoming guest fully stocked.

9.6 Upon termination of this Agreement, any unused Guest Consumables remaining at the Property may be removed by the Manager. The Manager is under no obligation to leave Guest Consumables at the Property, and the Owner is under no obligation to purchase them.

10. Cleaning

10.1 The Cleaning Fee is set by the Manager having regard to the size, configuration, and the cost of professional cleaning and linen for the Property. It is charged to the guest as a separate line item on each booking.

10.2 The Cleaning Fee is collected by the Manager and applied to the cost of turnover cleaning and linen. The Manager engages and pays the cleaning provider directly.

10.3 Cleaning Fees do not form part of Net Accommodation Revenue. The Management Fee is not charged on Cleaning Fees. This is a fundamental term of this Agreement.

10.4 Excess Cleaning Costs

Where the actual cost of cleaning a turnover exceeds the Cleaning Fee collected, the excess is deducted from gross accommodation revenue for that booking before calculation of Net Accommodation Revenue, and is therefore borne by the Owner and the Manager in proportion to their respective shares of Net Accommodation Revenue. This applies both where the excess arises from guest-caused damage or excess mess, and where it arises from periodic items such as rugs, windows, upholstery, or exterior cleaning.

  • (a) Excess Cleaning Costs are reported as a single aggregate figure on the monthly statement, with detail available on request;
  • (b) where the excess arises from guest conduct, the Manager will first seek recovery from the guest or the Booking Platform under clause 12.5, and will credit any recovery to the Owner and Manager proportionally; and
  • (c) deductions for Excess Cleaning Costs must not exceed $500 (incl. GST) per calendar month for the Property without the Owner's prior written approval.

10.5 The Manager will engage and manage the relationship with the cleaning provider, including scheduling, quality standards, and key handover.

10.6 The initial setup clean or deep clean of the Property is not a Cleaning Fee. It is an Initial Setup Cost, is an Owner cost, and is invoiced separately to the Owner at cost under clause 8.4.

11. Financial Reporting & Disbursements

11.1 The Manager will provide the Owner with a monthly statement within 10 business days of each month-end. Each statement will include:

  • (a) a summary of all bookings for the period, including guest names, check-in and check-out dates, and nightly rates;
  • (b) gross booking revenue received;
  • (c) Booking Platform fees and commissions deducted;
  • (d) any Excess Cleaning Costs deducted under clause 10.4, shown as a single aggregate figure;
  • (e) Net Accommodation Revenue;
  • (f) Management Fee calculated and deducted;
  • (g) any maintenance, repair, or other pass-through costs incurred;
  • (h) net amount disbursed to the Owner; and
  • (i) occupancy rate and average nightly rate for the period.

11.2 Cleaning Fees do not appear on the monthly statement, as they do not form part of Net Accommodation Revenue and are not a cost to the Owner. Records of Cleaning Fees collected and cleaning costs incurred are maintained by the Manager and are available to the Owner on request.

11.3 The Manager will maintain accurate financial records in respect of the Property and make them available to the Owner upon reasonable request.

11.4 Disbursements to the Owner will be made by electronic funds transfer to the bank account nominated by the Owner in the Owner Appointment Form.

12. Insurance, Damage & Liability

12.1 The Owner is responsible for maintaining adequate and current insurance for the Property, including building insurance, contents insurance, public liability insurance, and insurance suitable for short-term rental use. The Owner must notify the Manager immediately if any insurance policy lapses or is cancelled.

12.2 The Manager maintains professional indemnity insurance and public liability insurance appropriate to its business operations.

12.3 The Manager is not liable for any loss or damage to the Property or its contents caused by guests, beyond what is recoverable through Booking Platform host protection programs, security deposits, or guest damage insurance.

12.4 The Owner indemnifies the Manager against any claim, loss, damage, or liability arising from the Owner's failure to maintain insurance, comply with laws, or maintain the Property in a safe condition.

12.5 Guest Damage and Recovery

Where a guest causes damage to the Property, or leaves the Property requiring cleaning materially beyond a standard turnover, the Manager will:

  • (a) document the damage or excess cleaning with photographic evidence and notify the Owner within 48 hours;
  • (b) seek recovery from the guest and lodge a Booking Platform host-protection claim within the time limits set by the relevant platform;
  • (c) credit any recovery to the Owner, less any portion of the cost borne by the Manager; and
  • (d) keep the Owner informed of the progress of the claim.

The Manager does not guarantee recovery of any amount. Amounts not recovered are dealt with under clause 10.4 (in the case of cleaning) or as a pass-through expense (in the case of damage and repairs).

12.6 Neither Party will be liable to the other for any indirect, consequential, or incidental loss or damage arising under this Agreement.

13. Confidentiality

13.1 Each Party agrees to keep confidential all proprietary information, pricing strategies, business methods, guest data, financial details, and other confidential information disclosed by the other Party in connection with this Agreement.

13.2 This obligation survives termination of this Agreement and does not apply to information that is publicly available, independently developed, or required to be disclosed by law.

14. Termination

14.1 Termination by Notice

Either Party may terminate this Agreement at the end of the Initial Term or any Renewal Term by giving the other Party at least 90 days' written notice prior to the expiry of the then-current term.

14.2 Termination After Initial Term

After the Initial Term has expired, either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving the other Party 90 days' written notice. For the avoidance of doubt, this notice may be given at any point during a Renewal Term and is not limited to the end of a Renewal Term.

14.3 Termination for Material Breach

Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party commits a material breach of this Agreement and fails to remedy that breach within 14 days of receiving written notice specifying the breach and requiring it to be remedied.

14.4 Termination for Insolvency

Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if the other Party becomes insolvent, enters into voluntary administration, has a receiver or liquidator appointed, or is subject to any analogous event under applicable law.

15. Early Termination During Initial Term

15.1 If the Owner terminates this Agreement during the Initial Term other than pursuant to clause 14.3 (material breach by the Manager) or clause 14.4 (insolvency of the Manager), the Owner must pay the Manager an early termination fee of $1,000 (incl. GST).

15.2 The Early Termination Fee is:

  • (a) payable in addition to, and entirely separate from, any Onboarding Fee paid or payable under clause 8.3;
  • (b) payable in addition to, and entirely separate from, any commission on confirmed bookings payable under clause 16; and
  • (c) payable within 14 days of the effective date of termination.

15.3 Tier 2 Onboarding Recovery

Where Tier 2 is selected and the Owner terminates this Agreement during the Initial Term other than pursuant to clause 14.3 or 14.4, the Owner must also pay the Manager $2,200 (incl. GST), representing the onboarding costs incurred by the Manager at commencement of this Agreement. This amount is payable within 14 days of the effective date of termination and is separate from and in addition to the Early Termination Fee under clause 15.1.

15.4 For the avoidance of doubt, Initial Setup Costs invoiced to the Owner under clause 8.4 are the Owner's costs and are not refundable on termination, regardless of which Party terminates.

15.5 This clause applies only when the Owner terminates the Agreement. It does not apply if the Manager terminates the Agreement for any reason.

16. Commission on Confirmed Bookings After Termination

This is a material term of this Agreement. The Owner acknowledges that the Manager invests significant time and resources in securing bookings, and that confirmed bookings represent work already performed by the Manager.

16.1 If the Owner terminates this Agreement (whether during the Initial Term or any Renewal Term, and whether by notice under clause 14.1, clause 14.2, or otherwise), the Owner must pay the Manager the Management Fee on all confirmed bookings that were accepted by the Manager before the effective date of termination.

16.2 This obligation applies regardless of whether:

  • (a) the guest stays occur after the effective date of termination;
  • (b) the Owner hosts those guests themselves without the Manager's involvement;
  • (c) the Owner appoints a new manager or agent who hosts those guests; or
  • (d) the bookings are cancelled by the Owner, the new manager, or the guest after termination.

16.3 The Management Fee payable under this clause is calculated on the Net Accommodation Revenue that would have applied had the booking proceeded under this Agreement.

16.4 The Manager will, upon request, provide the Owner (or the Owner's new manager) with reasonable details of all confirmed bookings, including guest names, dates of stay, and expected revenue, to facilitate a smooth transition.

16.5 All amounts payable under this clause become due and payable within 14 days of the later of (a) the effective date of termination and (b) the date on which the relevant stay was scheduled to occur.

16.6 This clause applies only when the Owner terminates this Agreement. It does not apply if the Manager terminates the Agreement for any reason, including termination for the Owner's breach or insolvency.

17. Consequences of Termination

17.1 Upon termination of this Agreement by either Party, the Manager will cease marketing the Property and accepting new bookings from the effective date of termination.

17.2 All amounts owing by either Party to the other, including Management Fees, commission on confirmed bookings under clause 16, Early Termination Fees under clause 15, pass-through costs, and any other amounts, become due and payable within 14 days of the effective date of termination, unless a later date is specified in this Agreement.

17.3 Termination does not affect any rights, obligations, or liabilities that have accrued prior to the effective date of termination, including the Manager's right to fees on confirmed bookings under clause 16.

17.4 Clauses 9.6 (removal of Guest Consumables), 13 (Confidentiality), 16 (Commission on Confirmed Bookings After Termination), and 17 (Consequences of Termination) survive the termination of this Agreement.

18. Dispute Resolution

18.1 If a dispute arises in connection with this Agreement, the Parties agree to first attempt to resolve the dispute through good-faith negotiation within 14 days of one Party notifying the other of the dispute in writing.

18.2 If the dispute is not resolved by negotiation within 14 days, either Party may refer the dispute to mediation in Adelaide, South Australia, conducted by a mediator agreed upon by the Parties or, failing agreement, appointed by the Resolution Institute (or its successor body).

18.3 Neither Party may commence court proceedings in respect of a dispute arising under this Agreement until it has complied with clauses 18.1 and 18.2, unless the Party seeks urgent interlocutory relief.

19. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of South Australia. The Parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of South Australia and any courts competent to hear appeals therefrom.

20. Entire Agreement

20.1 This Agreement, together with the Owner Appointment Form, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties in relation to its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions, negotiations, representations, warranties, and arrangements, whether written or oral.

20.2 No variation of this Agreement is effective unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.

20.3 If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, that provision will be severed and the remaining provisions will continue in full force and effect.

20.4 No failure or delay by either Party in exercising any of its rights under this Agreement shall be deemed to be a waiver of that right, and no waiver of a breach of any provision shall be deemed to be a waiver of any subsequent breach of the same or any other provision.

21. Relationship of the Parties

Nothing in this Agreement shall create, or be deemed to create, a partnership or the relationship of employer and employee between the Owner and the Manager. The Manager operates as an independent contractor providing services to the Owner on the terms set out in this Agreement.

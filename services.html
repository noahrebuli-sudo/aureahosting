The guest experience others don't offer

Most short-stay managers hand over a key code and hope for the best. We don't.

Every Aurea guest receives a personalised boarding pass through Enso Connect, a streamlined digital guide covering everything they need before, during, and after their stay. From there, they can arrange services most listings simply don't offer.

Concierge check-in and checkout

A personal welcome on arrival, a private tour of the home, luggage assistance, and a curated arrival platter with complimentary wine. Checkout is just as considered. We handle the bags, load the dishwasher, sort the rubbish, and take care of every small task so guests leave without the stress of a standard STR departure.

Private chef experiences

Guests can arrange in-home dining through us. No restaurant booking required.

Flexible timing, made easy

Early check-in and late checkout are available where the calendar allows. Guests can request these directly through their boarding pass, and when approved, the additional revenue flows back to you as the owner.

Why this matters for your returns

Better guest experiences generate better reviews. Better reviews drive higher search placement on Airbnb and Booking.com. Higher placement means more bookings at stronger nightly rates. The guest experience is not a courtesy. It is a revenue lever.

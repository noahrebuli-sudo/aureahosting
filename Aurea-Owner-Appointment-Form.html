Fee Structure

Select your agreed management fee tier:

Tier 1: 24% Management Fee on Net Accommodation Revenue + $2,200 Onboarding Fee Tier 2: 26% Management Fee on Net Accommodation Revenue. No Onboarding Fee

All Management Fee rates and the Onboarding Fee are inclusive of GST.

The Management Fee is calculated on Net Accommodation Revenue, being gross accommodation revenue after deduction of the booking platform fees attributable to it, and after deduction of any Excess Cleaning Costs under clause 10.4 of the Agreement. Cleaning Fees do not form part of Net Accommodation Revenue and are not commissionable.

Cleaning

The Cleaning Fee is charged to the guest as a separate line item on each booking. Aurea Hosting engages and pays the cleaning provider directly. The Cleaning Fee is not commissionable and does not appear on the monthly statement, because it is not a cost to the Owner.

Where the actual cost of cleaning a turnover exceeds the Cleaning Fee collected for that booking, whether from guest conduct or from periodic items such as rugs, windows, or upholstery, the excess is deducted from gross accommodation revenue for that booking before the Management Fee is calculated.

Aurea Hosting and the Owner share this excess proportionally, so Aurea Hosting's fee is reduced too. It is reported as a single aggregate figure on the monthly statement, and is capped at $500 (incl. GST) per month for the Property without the Owner's prior written approval. Where the excess arises from guest conduct, Aurea Hosting seeks recovery from the guest first.

What Is and Is Not Included

Included at no cost to the Owner

  • Guest consumables. Aurea Hosting supplies and pays for all guest consumables, including coffee pods, tea, sugar, toiletries, toilet paper, paper towel, bin liners, and dishwashing and laundry products. These are replenished at every turnover at no cost to the Owner.
  • Listing creation and optimisation, pricing configuration, and guest welcome materials (Tier 1: covered by the Onboarding Fee. Tier 2: no separate charge).
  • Guesty software and back-end operational costs.

Owner costs, invoiced separately

Professional photography and the initial setup clean (deep clean) of the Property are an Owner cost. They are NOT covered by the Onboarding Fee and are NOT covered by the Management Fee. This applies to Tier 1 and Tier 2 equally.

  • Aurea Hosting coordinates both, engages the providers, and invoices them to the Owner at cost, with no margin, markup, or coordination charge.
  • A written estimate will be provided to the Owner before any provider is engaged. Payable within 14 days of invoice.
  • The initial setup clean is separate from the ongoing per-booking cleaning fee.
  • Durable items remain an Owner cost: mattresses, pillows, mattress protectors, pillow protectors, crockery, cutlery, cookware, appliances, and furnishings.
  • Ongoing maintenance and repairs are pass-through expenses at cost.
